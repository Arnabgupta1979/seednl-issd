YOUTUBE TITLE
Many routes to the farmer (pilot explainer, fictional farmer)

DESCRIPTION
A short illustrated explainer from a proposed SeedNL x WSER course on seed sector transformation. It follows a fictional farmer as she weighs five routes to seed and introduces the eight sector functions. Pilot for review; not a published course.

SUNO PROMPT
Instrumental underscore, 80 BPM, warm and unhurried. Soft plucked strings and a gentle kalimba-like melody over sustained pads and light hand percussion, mostly major with one thoughtful lift in the middle. No vocals, no risers, no heavy bass. Gentle build to a hopeful close, then a soft fade. About 80 seconds.
