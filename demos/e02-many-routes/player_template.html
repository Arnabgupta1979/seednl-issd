How to record
  1. Load the narration so the picture follows it. Check the scene changes by scrubbing the timeline.
  2. Open your screen recorder and choose this browser window or tab. Include system audio if you want the voice captured too.
  3. Click Record mode. The page goes full screen and counts down 5 seconds beside the stage.
  4. The animation plays once. When the side message says Finished, stop the recorder.
  5. Crop to the landscape stage and export at 1920 × 1080. Or record silently and lay the narration and music in your editor.
