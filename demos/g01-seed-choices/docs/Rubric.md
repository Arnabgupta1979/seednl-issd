# G01 feedback rubric

No points, totals, probabilities or yields.

- Five needs: timing; cash and cost; fit to crop and wishes; quality information; reach and quantity.
- Four levels, always symbol plus words: ● meets the need, ◐ partly meets, ○ does not meet well, ? not known.
- Each farmer has a different importance (matters most / matters / matters less) per need.
- "Together" (main + back-up): well covered if any chosen route meets the need; partly if any partly meets it; not known if only unknowns remain; otherwise still open.
- "Needs not fully covered" lists needs marked "matters most" whose combined cover is not "well covered".
- Stress test: holds up, under strain, exposed, or depends (ask). Described in words.
